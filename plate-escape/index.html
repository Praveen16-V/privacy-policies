Plate Escape — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Plate Escape is a food-themed puzzle game for Android made by Praveen Velumani ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information. The rules for using the game are in the Terms of Use.

The short version: there are no accounts, and your progress is stored on your phone. Analytics are off unless you opt in. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Purchases and optional cloud save go through Google.

Information stored on your device

  • Completed levels, coins, boosters, street renovations, cosmetic choices and settings
  • Your ad-consent state and, if you opt in, a random analytics ID and a local playtest log

This stays on your phone unless you use the optional features below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings deletes it, including progress that was never synced to Google Play Games.

Optional playtest analytics

Analytics are off until you choose "Allow playtest analytics". If you do, the game creates a random installation ID and records session dates, level attempts, completions, restarts, abandoned levels, tutorial steps and use of game features. The local log is capped at about 12,000 events. The game sends these events to GameAnalytics, whose SDK also collects standard device and network information (such as device model, operating system version, country and IP address) and may queue events and send them later when you are online. We use this to tune difficulty and see whether players come back.

We do not ask for your name or email, record where you touch the screen, or link the analytics ID to your Google Play Games account.

To stop, go to Settings → Privacy & playtest data. This stops further analytics and erases the local log and ID on your phone. Data already sent to GameAnalytics is not removed by this. To request its deletion, email us the analytics ID shown in Settings before you turn analytics off.

GameAnalytics privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional ads between won levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location it suggests
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps
  • Crash and diagnostic data about the ads SDK, used to measure performance and prevent ad fraud

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent screen is shown before ads are initialised. You can reopen your ad privacy choices at any time in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. Rewards from a rewarded ad are given only after the ad confirms completion. The "Remove ads" purchase removes between-level ads.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as Remove ads, the Starter bundle and booster packs) are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your card details or billing address. The game uses product IDs, purchase state and hashed purchase tokens to deliver and restore what you bought and to avoid giving the same reward twice. Optional cloud saves include your purchased entitlements and those hashed records, not payment details or raw purchase tokens.

Optional cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you connect Google Play Games, your game progress and a save timestamp are stored in Google's Saved Games service, linked to your Google account. The game does not request or record your Google account name, email or profile. Analytics consent and ID, sound settings and the local playtest log are not part of the cloud save.

You can disconnect in Settings. Disconnecting does not delete the cloud copy. Manage or delete Play Games data through your Google account and Play Games settings, or email us for guidance. Restoring an older snapshot can bring back an older balance of coins and boosters, so choose carefully if the game asks which save to keep.

Exports and support

Exporting a playtest log is something you do on purpose: the game writes a file where you choose and does not email or upload it. Share it only with whoever asked you for it. If you email us, we receive whatever you put in the message.

Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, purchases, optional cloud save and optional analytics
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads

The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

What Plate Escape does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords
  • No selling of your data to anyone
  • No analytics at all unless you opt in

Security and transfers

The game's service SDKs connect over encrypted connections. Google and GameAnalytics may process data in countries other than yours. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Plate Escape is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data. We hold very little: the only data we can look up is an analytics ID, if you opted in. To delete it, email support@toolsila.com with the ID shown in Settings → Privacy & playtest data; we will delete it within 30 days and reply when done. Everything else is on your phone (uninstall or clear storage), or with Google (your advertising ID, Play purchases and Saved Games) under Google's own controls.
