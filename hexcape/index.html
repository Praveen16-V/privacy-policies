Hexcape — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Hexcape is a single-player puzzle game made by Praveen Velumani. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it does not collect personal information about you, and it does not contain advertising or analytics of any kind.

Information stored on your device

Hexcape saves your game progress locally on your device. This includes which levels you have unlocked, your stars and best times, your selected companion, your daily streak, and your Trail records. It also saves your settings, such as sound, vibration, difficulty, theme, and accessibility options. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere unless you turn on the optional backup described below.

Uninstalling the game removes this local data.

Optional progress backup (Google Play Games)

Hexcape offers an optional backup so your progress can move between your devices and survive a reinstall. It is off by default. Nothing is uploaded, no sign-in is attempted, and no account information is requested unless you tap "Back up progress" in the game's Settings.

If you turn it on, you sign in with your existing Google account through Google Play Games Services, and the game saves a single snapshot of your game progress to your own Google account. That snapshot contains only:

  • Which level you have unlocked
  • Per-level results: stars, best tap count, best time, and difficulty
  • Trail records: best distance, lifetime distance, and number of sessions
  • Your daily streak and best streak
  • Your selected companion and which tutorial lessons you have seen

It contains no name, no email address, no location, no contacts, no device identifiers, and no advertising identifiers.

This snapshot is stored in your own Google account by Google Play Games Services. It is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, not by Praveen Velumani. The developer has no server, receives no copy of your save, and cannot read your progress.

Your purchase status is deliberately not included in the backup. Google Play is the record of what you own, and the game re-checks it with Google Play on each launch.

Purchases

Hexcape offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full campaign, the whole Trail (the first 50 metres of every walk are free), and all companions. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system. The developer never sees or receives your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information — only whether the purchase is owned.

Sharing a result

After an Endless walk or a cleared daily board, you can tap "Share" to send a picture of your result. The picture is drawn on your device and handed to your phone's own share menu, where you choose the app and the recipient. Nothing is sent unless you complete the share yourself, and nothing is sent to the developer. The picture shows only your distance or tap count, the date, your streak, and your companion — no name or account details.

What Hexcape does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No third-party trackers or social media SDKs
  • No accounts, sign-ups, passwords, or profiles created by the game
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files
  • No selling or sharing of any data with anyone

The full game, including every level, is playable offline.

Children

Hexcape is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains no advertising, no chat, and no social features.

Deleting your data

  • Local progress: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device's app settings.
  • Backed-up progress: open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings, and delete the saved game data for Hexcape. You can also delete Play Games data for your account at myaccount.google.com.
  • You may also email the address below to request deletion, and the request will be answered within 30 days.
