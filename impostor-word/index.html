Impostor Word — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Impostor Word is a party word game made by Praveen Velumani. Everyone gets the same secret word except the impostor, and the group has to find them. You can play with friends on one phone passed around the room, or online with each player on their own phone. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

The short version: the game has no accounts and keeps your game data on your phone. Online play sends only what a match needs to the game server. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.

The rules for using the game, including online play, are in the Terms of Use.

Information stored on your device

Impostor Word saves the following locally on your phone:

  • Player names you type in, scores, and lifetime stats
  • Game settings, such as category, language, sound, vibration and theme
  • Word packs you create yourself
  • Categories and card designs you have unlocked, and whether you own Pro
  • Your online nickname and colour, and a random player ID created by the app

This information stays on your phone. It is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings, deletes it. You can also clear stats at any time in Settings → Reset stats.

Playing with friends (one phone)

Games played with friends on one phone do not need the internet. The game itself sends nothing you type (names, word packs or anything else) anywhere. If your phone is online, ads may still load while you use the app (see Advertising below).

Online play

When you choose Online, the app connects to the Impostor Word game server so you can play with others. It sends:

  • The nickname and colour you choose
  • A random player ID created by the app on your phone. It puts you back in your seat if your connection drops, and keys your leaderboard entry and any reports. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your Google account.
  • What you do in a match: room settings, clues you type, chat messages, ready taps, votes and guesses

Other players in the same room see your nickname, colour, clues and chat messages. Like any internet service, the server also sees your IP address while you are connected.

The game server keeps each room in memory only while it is running and deletes it when the room closes. It does not keep a log of clues or chat. Two things are stored:

  • Leaderboard: your player ID, your latest nickname and colour, and your points and wins from games with other people. It is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Reports: if another player reports you, the report stores your player ID, nickname and the clues and chat you sent recently, so it can be reviewed. If you report someone, it stores your player ID and nickname as the reporter. Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. A player who is reported can have their chat muted or their online play suspended.

Common swear words, links and phone numbers are masked automatically, and you can mute any player's chat. Please do not share personal information in your nickname or chat.

If you enter your own server address in Settings → Online, your online data goes to that server instead, under its operator's rules.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location it suggests
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as views and taps
  • Crash and diagnostic data about the ads SDK, used to measure performance and prevent ad fraud

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Ads that let you unlock a category or a card design only play when you tap to watch one.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases (Impostor Word Pro)

The optional Pro upgrade removes ads and unlocks every category. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees or receives your card details, billing address or any other payment information. The game only asks Google Play whether your account owns Pro, so the upgrade can be restored after you reinstall.

Permissions

  • Internet: for online play, ads and checking your Pro purchase
  • Advertising ID: required by Google AdMob to show ads

The game does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. Vibration and keeping the screen awake during a game do not collect any information.

What Impostor Word does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking by the developer
  • No crash reporting by the developer (the Google AdMob SDK may send its own crash and diagnostic data, see Advertising)
  • No selling of your data to anyone

Children

Impostor Word is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

To delete your online data (Impostor Word, by Praveen Velumani):

  1. Open Impostor Word and go to Settings → Delete my online data. This opens an email to support@toolsila.com with your player ID already filled in.
  2. Send the email. If you can't use the button, email support@toolsila.com and include the player ID shown under that setting.

Your data is deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply when it's done.

  • Deleted: your leaderboard entry (player ID, nickname, colour and scores) and every report made about you. On reports you made about other players, your player ID and nickname are removed.
  • Kept: if your chat is muted or your online play is suspended, that restriction and the player ID it applies to are kept until it ends, so it can't be avoided by asking for deletion. Reports you made about others stay, without your name, until they expire after 90 days.
  • On your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads, and see Google's privacy policy above.
  • You can also email the address below with any data request. It will be answered within 30 days.
