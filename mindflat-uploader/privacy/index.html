Mindflat Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Mindflat Uploader is a private tool made by Praveen Velumani to upload and schedule videos on his own YouTube channel, Mindflat Films. Only the channel owner uses it. This policy explains what it does with data.

YouTube API Services

Mindflat Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Data from Google is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Permission to manage the Mindflat Films YouTube channel, granted once by the channel owner through Google sign-in.
  • The videos it uploads itself, and their IDs, titles and publishing status.

It does not access, collect or store any information about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository owned by the channel owner, and it is used only to upload, schedule or delete the channel's own videos. Video IDs and their review status are kept in the same private repository. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google Account security page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the tool immediately, and its stored token becomes useless.
