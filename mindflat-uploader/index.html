Mindflat Uploader

Mindflat Uploader is a private tool made by Praveen Velumani for his own YouTube channel, Mindflat Films, which publishes short animated films.

What it does

  • Uploads finished Mindflat Films videos to the Mindflat Films YouTube channel as private videos, with their title, description, tags, captions and thumbnail.
  • Sends each video to the channel owner for review, and after he approves it, schedules it to be published on the channel. Rejected videos are deleted from the channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API only on the channel owner's own account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.
